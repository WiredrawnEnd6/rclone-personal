# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This is a personal rclone setup used by its owner to access their own
Google Drive from a Linux computer. It is not a public service.

**Data accessed.** The app requests access to the owner's Google Drive
through Google's OAuth system, solely to sync and mount the owner's files.

**Data collection and storage.** This app has no server and collects
nothing. Authorization tokens and file data stay on the owner's own computer.

**Data sharing.** No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third
party.

**Revoking access.** Access can be revoked at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
